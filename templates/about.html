Why we built PlannedOut

Most of us run our household money from a spreadsheet that's hard to keep up, or from an app that wants our bank login and SMS inbox. Neither answers the questions people actually ask: is this month adding to my savings, can I afford this, and how long would my money last?

PlannedOut keeps the speed of a spreadsheet, adds the analytics and projections you'd otherwise build by hand, and encrypts everything so that only you can read it.
