What we can and can't see

We can't see

Amounts, balances, account names, descriptions, categories, salary, or anything else you enter. It's encrypted in your browser with a key derived from your vault passphrase, which we never receive.

We can see

Your email address, when you sign in, how many records you have, each record's type (for example "transaction"), the month it belongs to, and when it changed.

If you lose your passphrase

Any one of your recovery keys unlocks your data. If you lose your passphrase and every recovery key, nobody can recover your data, including us.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account removes your account and every record immediately.
