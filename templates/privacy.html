Your money data is yours. We built it so we can't read it.

Most finance apps ask for your bank login, read your SMS, and store every transaction where their staff, partners and advertisers can reach it. PlannedOut works the other way round: everything you type is encrypted on your device before it leaves, and only you hold the key.

No bank linking · No SMS reading · No ads · No selling data, because we have nothing readable to sell.

What we can and can't see

We can't see encrypted

  • Amounts and balances
  • Account and bank names
  • Descriptions and notes
  • Categories and budgets
  • Salary, EMIs, investments
  • Projections and what-ifs

We can see needed to run the service

  • Your email address
  • When you sign in
  • How many records you have
  • Each record's type (e.g. "transaction")
  • The month a record belongs to
  • When a record last changed

The technical details

  • Each vault has a random AES-256-GCM data key that encrypts every record.
  • That key is wrapped with a key derived from your passphrase using PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations.
  • Recovery keys wrap the same data key independently, so any one of them can unlock your vault.
  • All encryption uses your browser's built-in Web Crypto API. Your passphrase and unwrapped key never leave your device.

You stay in control

If you lose your passphrase

Any one of your recovery keys unlocks your data. Keep them somewhere safe and offline. If you lose your passphrase and every recovery key, nobody can recover your data, including us. That's the trade-off that makes it private.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account removes your account and every record immediately.
