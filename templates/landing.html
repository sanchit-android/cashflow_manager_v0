How it works

  1. Sign in with Google or an email code, and set a vault passphrase only you know.
  2. Add your month: type, paste from Excel, or import a CSV / .xlsx / bank statement.
  3. Read the verdict: surplus or shortfall, where it went, and what's trending up.
  4. Plan ahead: project savings year by year and try what-ifs.
