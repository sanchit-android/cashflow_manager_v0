Log it and ask it in plain words

Type 450 swiggy hdfc yesterday, 230 uber and both rows are saved, categorised and flashed in your sheet, with one-tap Undo. Paste a bank SMS or UPI alert and it becomes a row, matched to your account by its last 4 digits.

Ask food this month vs october, top merchants, runway or what's left to pay and get an answer with a chart and a Show rows button. Pin any answer to Home and it updates every month.

The assistant runs on your device. Nothing you type is sent to ChatGPT or any other AI service.

How it works

  1. Start right away: no sign-up. Your data is encrypted and kept in your browser. Create a free account (Google or an email code) whenever you want backup and sync.
  2. Add your month: type a sentence like “450 swiggy”, paste a bank alert or rows from Excel, or import a CSV / .xlsx / bank statement.
  3. Read the verdict: free to spend, where it went, and what's trending up. Or just ask.
  4. Plan ahead: project savings year by year and try what-ifs.
